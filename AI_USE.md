# AI Use

This repository discloses both historical and current AI use.

## Where LLMs are used

The superseded v2.0 workflow used LLM citation-context classification. The
current v1.1 comparative corpus uses no LLM for corpus assembly, retrieval,
sentence-window reconstruction, quality screening, sampling, human gold
labels, or reliability statistics. The 87-item pilot sheets are blank and no
current human or model labels exist.

## Where the full disclosure lives

Historical model/version/prompt/schema disclosure belongs to the corresponding
v2.0 manuscript materials. This export documents the current deterministic
context and human-pilot pipeline.

## What you can do with this export

You can re-fetch source material under your own access rights, reconstruct the
explicit citation windows, prepare blank pilot sheets, and run the included
reliability tooling after independent human annotation.
